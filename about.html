ART SONG TRANSLATIONS

About Art Song Translations

Art Song Translations is a small independent project based in the United States. We publish free English translations of art song texts, line by line, so that singers, pianists and audiences can follow the poetry.

How the translations are made

Our translations are drafted with the help of AI tools and then checked line by line against the original poems, using historical dictionaries for old, dialect and poetic words. All original texts on this site are in the public domain.

We are not certified translators. Certification (for example ATA) covers legal and official documents and is not usual for song translations. Our translations are meant to help singers and audiences understand the text, not to replace a singer's own word-by-word study or IPA work.

Using them

You may print any translation here free of charge in concert and recital programs, with the credit "Translation: Art Song Translations".

Mistakes and missing songs

If you spot a mistake, or a song you are singing is missing, send us a message on our Ko-fi page or reply to any email from us. We fix mistakes quickly and add requested songs free.

For singers short on time we also make print-ready recital programs and write translations into your own score: ko-fi.com/artsongtranslations.
