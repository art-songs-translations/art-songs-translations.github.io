Lerchengesang

Song of the Larks · Johannes Brahms, Op. 70, No. 2 (published 1877)
Poem: Karl Candidus (1817–1872)

Ätherische ferne Stimmen,
Der Lerchen himmlische Grüße,
Wie regt ihr mir so süße
Die Brust, ihr lieblichen Stimmen!
Ich schließe leis' mein Auge,
Da zieh'n Erinnerungen
In sanften Dämmerungen
Durchweht vom Frühlingshauche.
Ethereal, distant voices,
the larks' heavenly greetings,
how sweetly you stir
my breast, you lovely voices!
I softly close my eyes;
then memories drift past
in gentle twilights,
wafted through by the breath of spring.
About the song. The distant song of larks stirs the listener; when he closes his eyes, memories drift past in soft twilight, touched by the breath of spring. Candidus printed the poem in his Vermischte Gedichte (Leipzig, 1869). 'Ätherisch' means ethereal, of the upper air.
