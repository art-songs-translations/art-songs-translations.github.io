Aubade

Dawn Serenade · Gabriel Fauré, Op. 6, No. 1
Poem: Louis Pomey (d. 1901)

L'oiseau dans le buisson
A salué l'aurore,
Et d'un pâle rayon
L'horizon se colore,
Voici le frais matin!
Pour voir les fleurs à la lumière
S'ouvrir de toute part,
Entr'ouvre ta paupière,
Ô vierge au doux regard!
The bird in the bush
has greeted the dawn,
and with a pale ray
the horizon takes on colour:
here is the fresh morning!
To see the flowers in the light
opening on every side,
half-open your eyelids,
O maiden with the gentle gaze!
La voix de ton amant
A dissipé ton rêve,
Je vois ton rideau blanc
Qui tremble et se soulève,
D'amour signal charmant!
Descends sur ce tapis de mousse,
La brise est tiède encor,
Et la lumière est douce,
Accours, ô mon trésor!
Your lover's voice
has dispelled your dream;
I see your white curtain
trembling and lifting,
charming signal of love!
Come down onto this carpet of moss,
the breeze is still warm
and the light is gentle;
come running, O my treasure!
About the song. A dawn serenade: birdsong and the first light, then the lover's voice, call the girl to wake and come down to him. The poem is by Louis Pomey (spelled 'Pommey' in the Hamelle edition); the text here rests on the score alone. Fauré repeats the last line of each stanza (not repeated here). 'Vierge' here is simply 'maiden'.
