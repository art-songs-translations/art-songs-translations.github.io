Sonntag

Sunday · Johannes Brahms, Op. 47, No. 3
Poem: Folk song, from Ludwig Uhland's collection Alte hoch- und niederdeutsche Volkslieder (1844)

So hab' ich doch die ganze Woche
Mein feines Liebchen nicht geseh'n,
Ich sah es an einem Sonntag
Wohl vor der Türe steh'n:
Das tausendschöne Jungfräulein,
Das tausendschöne Herzelein,
Wollte Gott, ich wär' heute bei ihr!
So all week long I have
not seen my pretty sweetheart;
I saw her one Sunday
standing at the door:
the thousand-times-lovely maiden,
the thousand-times-lovely little heart;
would to God I were with her today!
So will mir doch die ganze Woche
Das Lachen nicht vergeh'n,
Ich sah es an einem Sonntag
Wohl in die Kirche geh'n:
Das tausendschöne Jungfräulein,
Das tausendschöne Herzelein,
Wollte Gott, ich wär' heute bei ihr!
And so all week long
my laughter will not leave me;
I saw her one Sunday
going into church:
the thousand-times-lovely maiden,
the thousand-times-lovely little heart;
would to God I were with her today!
About the song. A young man has not seen his sweetheart all week, but seeing her on Sunday keeps him laughing. Brahms took the words from Ludwig Uhland's collection of old German folk songs (1844), where they are headed 'Sonntag'. 'Es' (it) stands for 'Liebchen', a neuter noun; 'tausendschön' means lovely a thousand times over. Brahms sings 'wollte Gott' twice and repeats the last line of each stanza.
