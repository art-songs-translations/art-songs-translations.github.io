Dein blaues Auge

Your Blue Eye · Johannes Brahms, Op. 59, No. 8
Poem: Klaus Groth (1819–1899)

Dein blaues Auge hält so still,
Ich blicke bis zum Grund.
Du fragst mich, was ich sehen will?
Ich sehe mich gesund.
Your blue eye holds so still,
I look right to the bottom.
You ask me what I want to see?
I see myself healed.
Es brannte mich ein glühend Paar,
Noch schmerzt das Nachgefühl:
Das deine ist wie See so klar
Und wie ein See so kühl.
A glowing pair burned me;
the after-feeling still hurts:
yours is clear as a lake
and cool as a lake.
About the song. The beloved's calm blue eyes heal the hurt left by another, burning pair. Groth printed the poem in the section 'Klänge' of his Hundert Blätter (1854). 'Ich sehe mich gesund' is literally 'I see myself healthy': looking into her eyes makes him well, though the words can also mean that he sees himself healed, mirrored in her eye. 'See' here is a lake.
