Prayer · Hugo Wolf, Mörike-Lieder, No. 28 (1888)
Poem: Eduard Mörike (1804–1875)

Herr! schicke was du willt,
Ein Liebes oder Leides;
Ich bin vergnügt, daß beides
Aus deinen Händen quillt.
Lord! send what you will,
something dear or something painful;
I am content that both
flow from your hands.
Wollest mit Freuden
Und wollest mit Leiden
Mich nicht überschütten!
Doch in der Mitten
Liegt holdes Bescheiden.
With joys, I pray,
and with sorrows, I pray,
do not overwhelm me!
But in the middle
lies sweet contentment.
About the song. A prayer for the middle way: the speaker accepts both joy and sorrow from God's hands, but asks not to be overwhelmed by either. 'Willt' is an old form of 'willst'; 'wollest' is a subjunctive of polite request, 'may you be pleased'; 'Mitten' is an old dative of 'Mitte', the middle; 'Bescheiden' is a noun here: modest contentment. Wolf repeats 'Doch in der Mitten', which we print once.
