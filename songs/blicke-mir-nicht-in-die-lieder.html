Do Not Look Into My Songs · Gustav Mahler, Rückert-Lieder (1901, published 1905)
Poem: Friedrich Rückert (1788–1866)

Blicke mir nicht in die Lieder!
Meine Augen schlag ich nieder,
Wie ertappt auf böser Tat.
Selber darf ich nicht getrauen,
Ihrem Wachsen zuzuschauen.
Deine Neugier ist Verrat!
Do not look into my songs!
I lower my eyes
as if caught in a wicked deed.
I myself do not dare
to watch them grow.
Your curiosity is betrayal!
Bienen, wenn sie Zellen bauen,
Lassen auch nicht zu sich schauen,
Schauen selbst auch nicht zu.
Wenn die reichen Honigwaben
Sie zu Tag gefördert haben,
Dann vor allen nasche du!
Bees, when they build their cells,
do not let anyone watch them either,
nor do they watch themselves.
When the rich honeycombs
have been brought by them into the light of day,
then you, before all others, shall taste!
About the song. The poet forbids anyone to watch his songs as they grow, just as bees build their cells unseen; when the honeycomb is finished, the beloved may taste it first. Rückert's title is 'Verbotener Blick' (forbidden glance). Mahler sings 'Wenn die reichen Honigwaben' where Rückert has 'Wann die reifen' (ripe), and 'Schauen selbst auch nicht zu' for 'selbst sich auch'; he also brings back the first line before 'Deine Neugier ist Verrat!'. Rückert's editions differ between 'vor allen' (before all others), which Mahler sets, and 'vor allem' (above all). 'Naschen' is to nibble at sweets.
