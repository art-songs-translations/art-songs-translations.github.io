Komm bald

Come Soon · Johannes Brahms, Op. 97, No. 5 (1885, published 1886)
Poem: Klaus Groth (1819–1899)

Warum denn warten
Von Tag zu Tag?
Es blüht im Garten,
Was blühen mag.
Why wait, then,
from day to day?
In the garden there blooms
whatever can bloom.
Wer kommt und zählt es,
Was blüht so schön?
An Augen fehlt es,
Es anzuseh'n.
Who comes and counts
what blooms so beautifully?
There are no eyes
to look at it.
Die meinen wandern
Vom Strauch zum Baum;
Mir scheint, auch Andern
Wär's wie ein Traum.
Mine wander
from bush to tree;
it seems to me that to others, too,
it would be like a dream.
Und von den Lieben,
Die mir getreu
Und mir geblieben,
Wärst du dabei!
And of the dear ones
who are true to me
and have stayed with me,
if only you were among them!
About the song. The garden is in full bloom, but no one comes to look at it; of all the friends who have stayed true, only 'you' are missing. Groth's own title is 'Komm bald!' (Come soon!). 'Wärst du dabei!' is a wish: if only you were among them. Brahms repeats 'wärst du dabei' at the end.
